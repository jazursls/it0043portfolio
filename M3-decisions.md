I have 11 design tokens in my CSS stylesheet and they are all quite self-explanatory

4 of them are simply color tokens which I used for stuff that are universally seen in my portfolio site, like background color and text color. Though with those 4 tokens, there are only really 2 colors, I still decided to separate them into either background or text to overall just help me be more organized when I do the main stylesheet, and to have it be easily editable in the future if need be.

3 of the tokens are spacing tokens, or tokens I generally would use if I need some spacing stuff, they increase by 8 pixels, starting from 8 pixels up until 24.

4 of the last tokens are simply text sizes, to keep things uniform throughout my portfolio site, they also increase by 8 pixels, starting from 8 until 32.
